# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Broad digital wellness audience — students fighting social media distraction and working adults trying to reclaim focus. Context: someone who already feels they use their phone too much and is actively looking for a tool to help. They arrive on the site skeptically; they've tried other app timers and found them too harsh or too simple. They need to trust TimerX before they download.

## Product Purpose

Market the TimerX Android app and convert visitors to Google Play Store downloads. The site is not a product itself — it exists to earn trust and drive one action: download. Success = Play Store click-throughs. The site must demonstrate the app's personality (calm, non-punishing, focused) through its own design.

## Brand Personality

Sharp. Calm. Human.

Voice: Direct, confident, no buzzwords. "Set a timer. Get your time back." not "Leverage advanced analytics to optimize your digital wellness journey."

Emotional goal: The visitor should feel like the app understands them — not lectured, not shamed, just helped.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (navy, purple gradients, hero-metric rows, identical card grids)
- Crypto/NFT aesthetic (aggressive neons, chaos, overloaded glow effects)
- Kids' app visual language (primary colors, cartoon illustrations, bubbly shapes)
- Corporate productivity tools (dry, gray, no personality)

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** The site should feel focused and calm — not a page that demands attention with flashing CTAs and competing sections. If TimerX helps people escape distraction, the site shouldn't be distracting.
2. **Show, don't tell.** Let the phone mockup and visual motion demonstrate the app feels premium. Don't rely on "Fast Performance" bullet points.
3. **Earn trust before the ask.** The story (journey), the real FAQ, and honest copy must come before the download CTA. Don't lead with hype.
4. **Precision over decoration.** Every animated element should clarify or delight — not just move. Glow effects should accent, not flood.
5. **One voice throughout.** Every page — including Contact, About, Legal — should feel like the same brand. No orphaned pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Respect prefers-reduced-motion (all animations off or faded). Ensure sufficient contrast on dark backgrounds for body text. Touch targets ≥44px on mobile.
