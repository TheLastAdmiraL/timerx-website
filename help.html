Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to common questions. Click to expand.

What does TimerX do in a nutshell?

+
TimerX reduces doom-scrolling with per-app session timers and a brief Wait Screen before opening distracting apps. When a session ends, a gentle overlay nudges you to close—no harsh lock-outs.

How do I set a timer for an app?

+
Open TimerX → add the app → choose a session length and optional goals (minutes/day or number of opens). Use your phone normally— TimerX tracks sessions automatically.

What is the Wait Screen and can I customise it?

+
It’s a short pause you set (e.g., 5–15 s) before a target app opens. Add a motivational line or reminder—great for interrupting impulse taps.

Can I pause timers?

+
Yes. Use Pause for emergencies or deep work. Limits are temporarily disabled but your stats still record.

Which permissions are required?

+
Accessibility and Usage Access—so TimerX can detect the foreground app and show overlays at the right time. We don’t read your screen content.

What data do you collect?

+
Minimal analytics to improve TimerX (crashes, feature adoption). See our Privacy Policy.

How do I upgrade to Premium?

+
Open the Premium tab in the app to view plans and subscribe. Premium unlocks advanced analytics, weekly insights, and multi-device sync.

I’m stuck—how do I get help?

+
Use the Contact page and include your device model, Android version, and a short description.

Still need a hand?

We reply on weekdays, usually within 24–48 hours.
