Our story

From distraction
to clarity.

Every product has a story. Ours began with small frustrations, late-night conversations, and the relentless urge to build something better.

01

It started during quarantine

When lockdown hit, we quickly realized our phones had become constant companions. Endless scrolling was the new normal — even a few minutes away from the feed felt impossible. We knew something had to change, but no existing tool spoke to why we were stuck in that loop.

02

We tried everything

From simple timers to full-blown app lockers, we tested them all — yet each one felt like a band-aid. They would freeze our screen or count down seconds, but offered no context about why we were really here. We craved a tool that didn't just say "stop," but helped us remember what to do instead.

03

Then came the idea

One evening we asked: what if every enforced pause reminded us of our real goals? Instead of a blank lock screen, we could surface tasks, habits, and motivational prompts. That light-bulb moment sparked the core concept behind TimerX's unique productivity-first approach.

04

Building something better

We sketched, prototyped, and iterated — combining code with user feedback at every step. Our wait-screen became a digital nudge, surfacing your tasks and habits precisely when you need it. Today, TimerX empowers you to reclaim your attention and focus on what truly matters.

And we're just getting started.
