What it does

Built differently

Most screen-time apps just lock you out. TimerX makes you think.

New

Ask AI Reason Unlock

When your timer runs out, TimerX asks why you want to open the app. A good reason earns you more time. No good reason? You get a nudge to close it.

Pre-open Wait Screen

Before a monitored app launches, a brief pause gives your brain a moment to reconsider. 5 seconds can break a habit loop.

Usage Analytics

See daily and weekly breakdowns of every app you track. Spot patterns, measure improvement, stay honest with yourself.

Privacy First

Everything stays on your phone. We use Accessibility Service only to show overlays — we never read screen content or personal data.

About the Accessibility permission

TimerX needs Android's Accessibility Service to detect when you open a monitored app and show overlays at the right moment. It's the only way to do this without root access.

  • ✓All your app data and timers stay on your device
